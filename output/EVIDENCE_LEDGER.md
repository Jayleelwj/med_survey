# Evidence Ledger for the IEEE MS-MRI Survey

**Scope.** This ledger records the quantitative or high-risk factual claims retained for the survey revision. It is a claim-level audit, not a meta-analysis. Counts are reported in the unit used by the source; scans, sessions, image volumes, scan pairs, and patients are not treated as interchangeable. `NR` means not reported or not verified from the version-of-record material reviewed for this revision; it never means zero.

**Evidence levels.** `L0` = dataset/resource description without an evaluated model; `L1` = internal resampling or cross-validation; `L2` = locked same-program holdout or hidden benchmark; `L3` = external-site, external-scanner, geographic, or temporal validation; `L4` = prospective evaluation or a reader/workflow study in which human use is measured; `L5` = demonstrated patient outcome, clinical net benefit, or cost-effectiveness. A study may contribute more than one level, but the level shown below applies only to the stated claim.

## Interpretation Rules Used in the Manuscript

1. Performance values in different rows must not be ranked because the cohorts, lesion definitions, prevalence, sequence inputs, and validation designs differ.
2. A hidden challenge test (`L2`) is stronger than development cross-validation for the same benchmark, but it remains distribution-specific.
3. Multicenter training is not external validation unless a center, scanner, geography, or time period is held out from all model and threshold development.
4. Reader-time reduction (`L4`) is workflow evidence, not patient benefit (`L5`). No retained study in this ledger demonstrates an `L5` endpoint.
5. Dataset counts are reported exactly as patients, scans, sessions, scan pairs, or image volumes. No conversion is made between these units.
6. Whenever a numerical claim cannot be paired with the cohort, case condition, metric, and validation level, the manuscript should use a cautious qualitative statement or `NR`.
